# Cross-Border Readiness

Evaluate each candidate market separately:

- TikTok Shop and account availability as of a stated date;
- buyer demand and competition evidence;
- company, tax, customs, product, content, and consumer obligations requiring verification;
- payment, currency, pricing, margin, and settlement exposure;
- fulfillment, delivery promise, returns, and customer support;
- product and content localization;
- local creator and media plan;
- pilot budget, inventory, milestones, exit conditions, and owner.

Do not treat a country name as sufficient scope. Confirm site, seller model, fulfillment mode, product category, and launch date. Recommend local legal or tax review when material obligations remain uncertain.
