# Diagnostic Tree

Start from the business outcome and descend only where evidence supports it.

## Outcome

- Revenue = qualified traffic × conversion × average order value.
- Contribution = revenue minus product, platform, payment, creator, ad, fulfillment, logistics, return, and other variable costs.
- Cash and service outcomes depend on inventory, lead time, cancellation, delivery, and return behavior.

## Diagnostic branches

1. **Opportunity**: category/product demand, lifecycle, competition, creator/video evidence.
2. **Discovery**: search visibility, content reach, creator distribution, ads, live traffic.
3. **Conversion**: listing clarity, offer, proof, video-to-product alignment, checkout or policy friction.
4. **Economics**: contribution, commission, ads, discounts, returns, fulfillment.
5. **Operations**: stock cover, lead time, fulfillment, delivery, cancellation, returns, support.
6. **Risk**: compliance, IP, content, claims, account and market rules.

## Priority score

Do not hide judgment in a magic score. Show impact, confidence, effort, cash, learning speed, reversibility, and dependency separately, then explain the ranking.

Every initiative needs baseline, target, owner, start date, decision date, metric, guardrail, and stop/scale condition.
