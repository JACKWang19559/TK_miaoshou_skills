# New Store Launch and First-Order Plan

## Stage routing

- **Not opened**: verify target site, seller model, category eligibility, identity/tax/logistics requirements, and launch readiness before product tactics.
- **Opened, not listed**: select a small test assortment; validate category, compliance, unit economics, fulfillment, and complete listings.
- **Listed, zero orders**: establish whether qualified exposure, product clicks, add-to-cart intent, or checkout conversion is the first observable gap. Do not diagnose a cause from zero orders alone.
- **Early orders**: identify which product, offer, content, creator, ad, or live source produced contribution-positive orders and test repeatability.

## Compact beginner intake

Ask at most one compact batch before producing the first useful response:

1. Which TikTok Shop site/market?
2. Is the shop unopened, opened but not listed, listed with zero orders, or already receiving some orders?
3. What product/category will be sold, and are products selected/listed already?
4. What first-month test budget and sellable inventory are available?
5. Can the team produce short videos, work with creators, run ads, or host live sessions? Record unavailable channels without judging them.

If the user cannot answer budget or inventory precisely, accept a range. Continue with assumptions clearly labeled instead of blocking.

## Readiness gates

Before traffic spending, verify only what is material to the next test:

- exact site/account/category capability and current platform constraints;
- product and content compliance blockers;
- contribution floor using the site's Miaoshou pricing template/default settings;
- listing identity, category/attributes, SKU integrity, first image, core benefit, proof, offer, and buyer language;
- available inventory, lead time, fulfillment, delivery promise, returns, and customer support;
- at least one executable content or traffic route.

Route rule facts to `miaoshou-erp-tiktok-platform-rules`, risk triage to `miaoshou-erp-tiktok-compliance-protection`, pricing to `miaoshou-erp-tiktok-profit-operations`, and listing proposal to `miaoshou-erp-tiktok-listing-optimize`.

## First-order experiment

Define the smallest testable combination:

`one product or tight product group × one clear offer × one buyer problem/proof angle × one primary traffic source`

Use market evidence through `miaoshouerp-tk-product-selection` when available. Do not present an unverified product, viral format, creator, or benchmark as proven.

Choose one primary route based on capability:

- **Content-capable, low budget**: repeatable short-video tests linked to a complete listing.
- **Creator-capable**: small evidence-based creator sample with explicit economics and content rights.
- **Ad-capable with validated creative/listing**: capped learning test with pause conditions; do not use ads to conceal an unready offer.
- **Live-capable with sufficient host, traffic, offer, inventory, and fulfillment**: small rehearsed live test.
- **No channel capability yet**: prioritize listing readiness plus creation of the first executable content/creator operating loop.

Do not prescribe all channels simultaneously.

## 7/14/30-day structure

### By day 7: become test-ready

- Confirm site/category/rule/compliance scope.
- Select the smallest test assortment.
- Establish contribution floor and inventory guardrails.
- Complete listing and produce the first test assets/briefs.
- Record baseline as zero/unknown rather than inventing benchmarks.

### By day 14: run controlled tests

- Launch the chosen content, creator, ad, or live experiment.
- Track exposure, qualified visits, product clicks, add-to-cart, orders, spend, contribution, cancellation, and stock impact as available.
- Change one meaningful variable at a time where practical.
- Pause on compliance, fulfillment, inventory, or contribution guardrail breach.

### By day 30: decide

- Scale a route only when orders show repeatability and contribution/inventory guardrails hold.
- Iterate when buyer interest exists but listing/offer evidence suggests a correctable conversion gap.
- Stop or replace the hypothesis when meaningful exposure produces no downstream intent, economics fail, or execution capacity is inadequate.
- Convert the first month into a 30/60/90-day growth roadmap once usable evidence exists.

## Beginner-friendly output

Explain what to do this week, what evidence to collect, how much can be risked, and the exact condition for continuing. Define metrics in plain language. Never promise a first order, virality, ROAS, or a universal posting quantity.
