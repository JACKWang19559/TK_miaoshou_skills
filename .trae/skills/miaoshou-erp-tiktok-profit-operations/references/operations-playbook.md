# Operations Playbook

Use a base scenario plus relevant stress cases: higher creator commission, higher return rate, higher ad ratio, candidate price, demand spike, or demand decay after a viral peak.

Do not change several assumptions at once unless clearly labeled as a combined stress case.

Prefer user-defined gates: minimum contribution, minimum margin, maximum commission, maximum break-even ad ratio, maximum cash exposure, reorder trigger, maximum stock cover, and return-rate stop condition. If absent, report mathematical facts and request the business guardrail instead of inventing a universal benchmark.
