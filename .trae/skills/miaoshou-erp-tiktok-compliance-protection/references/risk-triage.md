# Risk Triage

## Levels

- **Blocker**: current evidence indicates publication or continued activity should stop pending resolution.
- **High**: credible risk with material account, legal, financial, or customer impact; prioritize verification and remediation.
- **Medium**: plausible issue with incomplete evidence or limited impact.
- **Low**: housekeeping or monitoring item.
- **Unverified**: insufficient evidence; do not convert suspicion into accusation.

## Evidence checklist

Record source, capture date, site, product/content/account identifiers, screenshots or exports, transaction/order context where relevant, ownership or authorization documents, and a factual chronology. Separate original evidence from interpretation.

## Tracks

### Product and content

Check consistency, required disclosures, unsupported claims, regulated attributes, prohibited wording, image/video rights, and site-specific requirements.

### Brand and IP

Check ownership, authorization scope, exact protected asset, alleged use, seller/creator/content identity, price anomaly as a signal rather than proof, and preservation of evidence.

### Customer disputes

Check order facts, promised versus delivered state, communication timeline, policy window, requested remedy, and respectful factual wording. Do not admit unsupported liability.
