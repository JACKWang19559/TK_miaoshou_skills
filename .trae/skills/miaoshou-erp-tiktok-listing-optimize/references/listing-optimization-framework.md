# Listing Optimization Framework

## Audit dimensions

1. **Identity and accuracy**: product type, model, quantity, compatibility, material, size, and claims agree across title, description, attributes, images, and SKUs.
2. **Search relevance**: primary buyer term appears naturally; secondary terms are relevant; title remains readable; category and attributes support discovery.
3. **Value communication**: buyer problem, core benefit, proof, differentiator, use case, and package contents are clear.
4. **Visual conversion**: first image identifies the product; later images prove scale, use, details, variants, and included items; video demonstrates rather than merely asserts.
5. **Offer**: price, voucher, bundle, free-shipping, and urgency language remain truthful and economically viable.
6. **Trust**: reviews, UGC, warranty, delivery expectations, and after-sales statements are accurate and attributable.
7. **Mobile clarity**: the first title segment, first image, first description screen, and first video seconds carry the essential message.

## Priority

- **Blocker**: false or conflicting facts, wrong category, missing required fields, prohibited claim, broken SKU mapping.
- **High**: prevents discovery or understanding, weak first image, unclear offer, missing core benefit.
- **Medium**: secondary keyword, supporting visual, comparison, FAQ, social proof.
- **Optional**: stylistic refinement with uncertain incremental value.

## Field proposal

For every proposed change include:

| Field | Current | Proposed | Evidence | Reason | Confidence | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not collapse multiple uncertain facts into polished copy. Resolve facts first.
